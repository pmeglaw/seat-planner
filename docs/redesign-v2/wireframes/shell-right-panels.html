Phase 2 wireframe, slice 1. Spec: PHASE2UX.md §1.4. Decisions: D0-a, D0-b, D0-f (320px), D0-g (10 events + Show more to 25). One panel open at a time; anchored to its icon (icon outlined, bottom edge open into the panel); dark like the header; full height; floats over content; Esc or the icon closes and focus returns to the trigger.
