Motion — productive curves, one axis

standard cubic-bezier(.2,0,.38,.9) · entrance (0,0,.38,.9) · exit (.2,0,1,.9)
fast-01 · 70ms · marker hover, toggles
fast-02 · 110ms · hover colour, left panel
moderate-01 · 150ms · small expansion
moderate-02 · 240ms · panels, slot, tearsheets
slow-01 · 400ms · large expansion
slow-02 · 700ms · overlay dimming

Panels slide on one axis with the entrance curve and leave on the exit curve. Keyboard-initiated and hundred-times-a-day actions (marker hover, palette open) do not animate at all. Under prefers-reduced-motion the asset collapses every duration to 1ms — nothing here needs a second query.
