Status and marks

The two mark sets everything else is built around: the shell's mode indicator (PHASE2UX §1.5) and the seat status symbols (DECISIONS deviation 3). Each carries two signals in the mark itself and is checked on the surface it lands on when hovered or pressed, not at rest.
